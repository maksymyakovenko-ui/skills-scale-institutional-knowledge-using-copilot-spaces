# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA Lead / Test Manager

### Role Summary
The QA Lead defines the quality strategy for the project, ensures acceptance criteria are measurable, and coordinates validation across development, integration, and release stages.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, quality gates, and regression priorities
- Review acceptance criteria for testability and completeness
- Coordinate manual QA, automation planning, and defect triage
- Partner with developers and PMs to confirm release readiness
- Track quality trends and escalations across milestones

### Goals
- Reduce defect escape risk and improve confidence before release
- Align testing work with project scope and timelines
- Make quality expectations explicit and measurable

### Typical Communication
- Test plans, signoff checkpoints, and release readiness reviews
- Defect triage with engineering and PM stakeholders
- Weekly quality updates during sprint and release cycles

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Developers to clarify test coverage and defect priority.
- Supports Product Managers by validating whether acceptance criteria are sufficiently objective.
- Coordinates with Project Managers to ensure quality checkpoints are included in the plan and release timeline.

---

## Security / Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
The Security / Compliance Lead ensures the project meets required security, privacy, and compliance expectations before delivery and during operations.

### Responsibilities
- Review security risks, dependencies, and vulnerabilities across the solution
- Confirm controls, policies, and data handling requirements are followed
- Coordinate privacy, compliance, and audit readiness activities
- Partner with engineering and release teams on risk mitigation and signoff
- Escalate critical issues and recommend remediation priorities

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk without slowing value delivery unnecessarily
- Ensure systems are reviewable, auditable, and resilient by default
- Create clear ownership for risk remediation and approval gates

### Typical Communication
- Security review checkpoints and remediation tracking
- Risk and compliance briefings for leadership and stakeholders
- Release gating conversations with PM and Release Manager

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Developers to review architecture, dependency risk, and remediation plans.
- Supports Project Managers by surfacing compliance constraints and escalation paths.
- Partners with Release Manager to confirm that deployment readiness includes security signoff.

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
The Release Manager coordinates delivery readiness, deployment windows, rollback planning, and communication for releases to production or other critical environments.

### Responsibilities
- Prepare release plans, cutoff dates, and deployment checklists
- Coordinate communication with engineering, support, and stakeholders
- Verify rollback and incident response readiness
- Track release quality gates and deployment outcomes
- Facilitate launch communications and post-release follow-up

### Goals
- Increase release confidence and reduce operational disruption
- Make release timing, readiness, and ownership clear across teams
- Improve consistency of deployment practices across projects

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and deployment approvals
- Stakeholder notifications and launch updates
- Incident and rollback coordination with on-call and leadership teams

### Interaction with existing roles
- Collaborates with Developers and QA Lead to confirm release criteria are met.
- Coordinates with Project Managers on timeline, risk, and communication planning.
- Aligns with Security / Compliance Lead to ensure policy and risk checks are complete before deployment.

---

## Customer Success / Stakeholder Advocate

### Role Summary
The Customer Success / Stakeholder Advocate ensures that real user needs, support feedback, and stakeholder priorities are visible in planning and delivery decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Capture customer feedback, support trends, and adoption concerns
- Represent stakeholder needs during prioritization and tradeoff discussions
- Help translate user pain points into operational or product requirements
- Support go-live readiness by validating expected user impact and communication needs
- Provide post-release feedback to inform iteration and improvement cycles

### Goals
- Keep delivery grounded in customer value and stakeholder outcomes
- Improve adoption, satisfaction, and supportability of project outcomes
- Ensure customer signals shape backlog prioritization and release decisions

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback reviews and stakeholder updates
- Roadmap and release conversations with Product and Project leads
- Support or adoption summaries for cross-functional teams

### Interaction with existing roles
- Feeds data and insight to Product Managers for prioritization and outcome measurement.
- Works with Project Managers to ensure stakeholder communications are aligned with delivery milestones.
- Gives Developers and QA Lead context on likely user impact, edge cases, and expected workflows.

---

## Technical Architect / Systems Lead

### Role Summary
The Technical Architect / Systems Lead provides guidance on system design, platform choices, integration patterns, and long-term maintainability so the team can deliver a coherent solution.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical direction, standards, and architectural guardrails
- Review integrations, dependencies, and platform constraints
- Help teams assess trade-offs between speed, scalability, and maintainability
- Support design reviews and major technical decisions
- Identify cross-team or platform risks that require escalation

### Goals
- Keep the solution aligned with platform standards and long-term technical health
- Reduce avoidable rework and maintainability issues
- Support delivery teams with clear technical decision-making frameworks

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews, design docs, and decision records
- Integration planning with engineering and project stakeholders
- Risk discussions tied to platform, scalability, or dependency decisions

### Interaction with existing roles
- Advises Developers on design quality and technical trade-offs.
- Helps Product Managers and Project Managers understand technical complexity, dependencies, and timing impacts.
- Works with Security / Compliance Lead and QA Lead to ensure technical choices support quality and governance requirements.

- Together, these roles clarify accountability across planning, delivery, release, stakeholder communication, and operational readiness.
